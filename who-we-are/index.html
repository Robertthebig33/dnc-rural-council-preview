Our mission

We are dedicated to empowering rural communities through organized, accessible, and civic-minded engagement.

We believe rural voices are essential to the Democratic Party and work tirelessly to ensure every rural voter has the tools and support needed to show up and be heard.

Four regions

From every corner of rural America

Regional representatives carry the council’s work across the Eastern, Midwestern, Southern and Western regions.
