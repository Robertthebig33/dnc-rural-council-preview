Our work areas

Three ways we organize

  1. 01 / 03

    Voter Outreach

    We bring the party to the people. Our outreach teams travel to small towns and farming communities to educate residents on their rights and how to register and vote.

  2. 02 / 03

    State Chapter Support

    We provide state chapters with the resources they need to stay strong. From training to strategic planning, we help rural Democrats organize and grow their presence.

  3. 03 / 03

    Local Candidates

    We empower local candidates with the tools to win. From grassroots organizing to media outreach, we provide the support needed to represent rural voices in Washington.

Our priorities

Advocating for rural America

We are dedicated to ensuring that the voices of rural communities are heard in the halls of power.

  • Agriculture

    Supporting farmers and ranchers through fair trade policies and sustainable practices for our future.

  • Broadband

    Connecting rural communities with high-speed internet to ensure access to education and modern healthcare.

  • Healthcare

    Advocating for affordable, accessible healthcare that meets the unique needs of our rural neighbors.

  • Economic Growth

    Investing in small-town economic development to create jobs and strengthen our local communities.

Tools for organizers

Training & resources

We provide the tools and training needed to organize your community. From voter outreach guides to local candidate support, we equip rural Democrats with the resources to show up and make their voices heard.
